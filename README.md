# Research Projects

## 01. 5G NR and Non-Terrestrial Networks

### Delay-Aware Uplink HARQ Process-Budget Analysis for GEO 5G-NTN

Research investigating uplink HARQ process constraints
under GEO satellite propagation delays using
OpenAirInterface RF simulation.

**Research areas:**

- HARQ process-budget analysis
- H16 and H32 HARQ configurations
- GEO propagation-delay effects
- HARQ process occupancy and scheduler behavior
- Uplink goodput and completion-time analysis
- Offered-load and channel-impairment experiments
- Statistical analysis and numerical evidence verification

The repository contains de-identified experimental data,
figure source tables, audit evidence, and verification
scripts. Complete private raw logs are not publicly released.

**Repository:** [5G-NTN-HARQ-Process-Budget-Analysis](https://github.com/tanjimahmed899/5G-NTN-HARQ-Process-Budget-Analysis)

### OpenAirInterface GEO NTN Experiments

Experimental investigations of GEO satellite-delay
conditions, HARQ ON/OFF operation, end-to-end connectivity,
TCP/UDP transport performance, and 5G NR network behavior.

**Repository:** [OAI GEO NTN Experiments](https://github.com/tanjimahmed899/oai-geo-ntn-experiments)

## 02. Quantum Communication and Computing

### Quantum Router Communication System

Circuit-level research integrating quantum routing,
quantum switching, and unitary quantum state transfer.

**Research areas:**

- Ten-qubit quantum communication architecture
- Controlled-SWAP quantum routing
- Quantum Controlling Switch
- Quantum state-transfer circuits
- Qiskit statevector and density-matrix simulation
- Quantum-state fidelity analysis
- Depolarizing noise evaluation
- Analytical and numerical validation

The research evaluates reconstructed quantum circuits
through numerical simulation rather than physical
quantum-hardware experiments.

**Repository:** [Quantum Router Communication System](https://github.com/tanjimahmed899/Quantum-Router-Communication-System)

### Quantum Teleportation Simulation

Research code and scientific figures related to
quantum teleportation and quantum communication.

**Repository:** [Quantum Teleportation Simulation](https://github.com/tanjimahmed899/quantum-teleportation-simulation)

## 03. Artificial Intelligence and Deep Learning

### Camera-Motion-Corrected Elephant Trajectory Forecasting

Research investigating computer vision and deep
learning methods for elephant trajectory forecasting
under moving-camera conditions.

**Research areas:**

- Transformer-based trajectory forecasting
- Camera-motion estimation and correction
- Object detection and multi-object tracking
- ByteTrack tracking framework
- Domain adaptation
- Conformal uncertainty quantification
- Average and final displacement errors
- Cross-dataset evaluation
- Synthetic virtual-boundary analysis

The repository contains retrospective experimental
evidence, research scripts, statistical results,
and scientific audits.

This work is not a field-deployed wildlife warning
system. Experimental limitations and negative transfer
findings are documented in the repository.

**Repository:** [Elephant Trajectory Research](https://github.com/tanjimahmed899/CP10-Elephant-Trajectory-Research)

---

## 04. Digital Signal Processing and Space Weather

### Solar Flare Detection and Short-Term Prediction

Research exploring causal wavelet-energy features
for solar flare detection and short-term prediction
using GOES X-ray flux measurements.

**Research areas:**

- Causal wavelet-energy reconstruction
- Discrete wavelet transform
- Adaptive percentile-based thresholds
- Time-series event detection
- GOES XRS-B measurements
- NOAA reference-event matching
- Detection and warning-lead evaluation

The public repository supports a documented
April-July 2022 benchmark reconstruction.
Full manuscript-wide reproduction is not claimed.

**Future research direction:** Machine learning,
deep learning, and neural-network-based extensions
of the existing signal-processing framework.

**Repository:** [Solar Flare Causal Wavelet](https://github.com/tanjimahmed899/solar-flare-causal-wavelet)
